Who We Are

Founded in 2020 and headquartered in London, DeeLit Group Limited is a fast-growing FMCG wholesale distributor specializing in sourcing and distributing top-tier global brands across Europe and international markets.

We bridge the gap between world-class product manufacturers and wholesale buyers, commercial retailers, and major e-commerce platforms. Our operations focus on key consumer categories including personal care, home appliances, power systems, and specialized consumer goods.
